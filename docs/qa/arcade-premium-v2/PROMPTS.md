## Desktop

Create a wide 16:9 composition, ideally 2048×1152. This is a quiet magical arcade portal environment, NOT a full forest wallpaper and NOT a UI screenshot. Very dark midnight navy and muted purple space; distant mushroom forest silhouettes only near outer left/right corners; soft distant deep-red mushroom forms low near the corners; a few faint cyan/violet spores; restrained emerald haze close to the lower outer edges; a very fine aged-gold incomplete portal arc near the perimeter. Deep edge vignette. The central 70% must stay almost-black navy, low contrast, calm, and readable behind two large dynamic game cards. Authored atmospheric illustration with subtle material/grain and carefully lit silhouettes, premium rather than a generic gradient. No text, no logos, no characters, no cards, no buttons, no participant data, no watermark. No saturated green forest, no large bright stars or mushrooms, no oversized golden frame, no noisy particles. This bitmap is the background source itself, not a concept board.
